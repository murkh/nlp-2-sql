# Domain Terms — Sales Management System

## Common Query Translations

### "Top products"
Refers to products ranked by total revenue (sum of line_total from order_items).
Can also mean by quantity sold depending on context.

### "Best sellers"
Products with the highest total quantity sold across all completed orders.

### "Customer churn"
Customers who have not placed any order in the last 6 months (180 days).
Identified by comparing their last order_date with the current date.

### "Active customers"
Customers who have placed at least one order in the last 90 days.

### "Average order value" (AOV)
Calculated as: total revenue / number of completed orders.
Uses `orders.total_amount` for completed orders only.

### "Sales growth" or "revenue growth"
Year-over-year (YoY) or month-over-month (MoM) comparison of total revenue.
Calculated as: (current_period - previous_period) / previous_period × 100.

### "Market share" by region
Percentage of total revenue contributed by each region.
Calculated by grouping orders by customer region.

### "Product mix"
Distribution of sales across product categories.
Uses `products.category` joined with `order_items`.

### "Customer lifetime value" (CLV)
Total revenue generated by a customer across all their completed orders.
Calculated as: SUM(orders.total_amount) WHERE status='completed' grouped by customer_id.

### "Repeat customers"
Customers who have placed more than one order.

### "Conversion rate"
In this context, refers to the ratio of completed orders to total orders.
Calculated as: COUNT(completed) / COUNT(all) × 100.
